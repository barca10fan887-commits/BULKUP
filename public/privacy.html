BULKUP

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

BULKUP helps you track food, training, body weight and supplements while you build muscle. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data you enter

You can record your profile (name, age, sex for calorie estimates, height, weight and goals), food entries, water, workouts, body measurements, supplement use, weekly check-ins, coach conversations and progress photos.

Where it's stored

  • Without an account: everything stays on your device. We can't see it.
  • With an account: your data is stored in our database (hosted by Supabase) so it syncs between your devices. Access rules ensure only you can read your records. Progress photos are kept in private storage and are only shown to you through short-lived links.

AI features

The AI coach, weekly AI reviews and food photo scanner are off until you turn on AI features. When you use them, the relevant data (for example your question, your targets, recent logs and the meal photo you scan) is sent to our AI provider to generate your answer. BULKUP currently uses Google's Gemini API. On Google's free tier, Google may use submitted content to improve its products and services; see Google's Gemini API terms. If BULKUP uses Anthropic's Claude instead, submitted content isn't used to train models. You can turn AI features off at any time in Settings > Privacy.

Food lookups

Food searches and barcode lookups query Open Food Facts, a public food database. Only the search words or barcode number are sent.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a barcode, photograph a meal or take a progress photo.

Notifications

Reminders are optional, scheduled on your device, and only sent after you give permission.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.
  • We don't use third-party tracking or analytics.

Your choices

  • Export your data from Settings > Your data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Your data. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Health information

BULKUP provides general fitness guidance. Calorie and nutrition figures are estimates and are not medical advice.
